When planning a website there’s actually more to it than I thought. You have to think about what the website is for, who is going to use it, and what will make people want to stay on the site. What the client wants or needs. What graphics are wanted or need to be used.Do they need multiplatform, what colors are wanted to be used. Lastly accessibility, timeline and most important the budget. All these things should be considered before every writing one single line of code.
